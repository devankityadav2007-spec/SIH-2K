The Healthcare Challenge

Hospitals operate in isolation, leading to resource imbalances and inefficient patient care during critical situations.

Resource Scarcity

Critical shortages of ICU beds, oxygen cylinders, and medicines during peak demand while other hospitals have excess resources unused.

Real-Time Data Gap

Lack of real-time visibility into resource availability across hospitals, causing delays in emergency response and patient transfers.

Poor Communication

Limited communication channels between hospitals prevent effective coordination and resource sharing during emergencies.

Transport Challenges

Inefficient transportation of resources between hospitals due to lack of logistics coordination and tracking systems.

Overburdened Facilities

During crises, some hospitals become overwhelmed while others remain underutilized, leading to uneven patient care quality.

High Costs

Unnecessary duplication of expensive equipment and resources increases operational costs across healthcare facilities.
